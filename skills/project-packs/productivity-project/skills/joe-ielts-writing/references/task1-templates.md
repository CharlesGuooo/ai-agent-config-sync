# Task 1 模板句库（小作文的核心资产）

> 小作文重模板句——这是和大作文最根本的区别。大作文教"想清楚"，小作文教"套得准"。
> Joe 的原话：记汉语主干，每句只有 4-5 个汉字的事儿。

## 主语构造（小作文唯一没有模板的地方）

考试背景一直在变，唯独主语要自己造。**搞定主语构造 = 看懂这张图**。

**三步流程**：

1. **看数据轴**（有数据的轴，纵或横）：确认是**比例**还是**数量**
   - 比例：proportion / percentage / figure
   - 数量：the number of + 可数名词 / the amount of + 不可数名词
2. **结合标题**：最终主语一定是"**什么什么的比例**"或"**什么什么的数量**"——只有比例或数量才能上升、下降、稳定、波动
3. **翻译标题**：遇到"A 介词 B 介词 C"结构，**从后往前倒着翻**（B 的 A）
   - 例：Percentage of households in Great Britain with car → 有车的家庭 → **有车的家庭的比例**

完整流程图见 `../assets/task1/subject-construction-flow.png`

**四个主语词的禁区**：
- number：只接可数名词，不能描述百分比
- amount：只接不可数名词，不能接 people
- proportion：只描述百分比
- figure：万能——可数/不可数/国家/百分比都行（the figure for Canada / unemployment）

## 基础模板句（主 + 动 + 副 + 数据 + 时间）

> The [主语] [动词] [副词], rising/falling from [数据1] to [数据2] between [时间1] and [时间2].

示例：The proportion of mobile phone users increased dramatically, rising from 55% to 80% between 2010 and 2015.

**动词替换**：上升 increased / rose / climbed / grew；下降 decreased / declined / dropped / fell；稳定 remained/stayed/kept stable；波动 fluctuated（杂乱）/ waved（匀称）

**幅度副词**：剧烈 dramatically / sharply；大 significantly / considerably / substantially；缓慢 slightly；逐渐 gradually；稳定 steadily

## 四种句型变化（一句汉语，四种英文）

记汉语主干，每句 4-5 个汉字：

1. 比例增长：The proportion of X increased dramatically.
2. 有一个增长在……上：There was a dramatic increase in the use of X.
3. 一个增长在……上出现了：A considerable increase in X occurred.
4. 比例呈现……趋势：The proportion of X saw/showed an upward trend.

**复杂句加长**：There was a significantly upward trend with a fluctuation in the number of X from 23% to 78% between 2000 and 2010.（trend + with + 波动 + from...to + between...and）

## 高级模板句（6 分流水线账 → 7 分的分水岭）

**模板 9：先增后减 / 先减后增（V 字）**

> The cost had increased to 23% in 1991 before decreasing to 9% at the end of the period.

语法注意：整体时间在过去 → 前半句用**过去完成时**（had increased）；before 是介词 → 后面动词用 **doing**（decreasing）。

**模板 10：整体局部（对勾）**

> The share price saw a dramatic increase from $35 in 1996 to $80 in 1998, in spite of a noticeable decrease in 1997.

思维方式：只要**整体和局部不一样**就能套——整体上升，尽管中间下降/波动/水平。升级版用 though 接句子：...grew steadily from 38 billion to 61 billion, **though the growth slowed** over the last two years.

**切线思维（使用高级模板的前提）**：对每根线进行切线——几句就切"句数 − 1"下（2 句切 1 下、3 句切 2 下、4 句切 3 下），每处切线套一个高级模板。以模板为指导，挑选数据——不是看到什么写什么。

## 特殊变化句

- 成倍增长：The proportion of elderly people almost doubled/tripled, rising from 9% in 2000 to 16% in 2005.
- 减少一半：The number of wild birds in the UK halved, declining from 5 million to 2.5 million.
- 保持不变：Between 1995 and 2000, the amount of goods transported by road remained relatively stable, at approximately 70 million tons.
- 起伏波动：The number of prisoners in the US fluctuated wildly, ranging between 80,000 and 120,000 from 1950 to 1980.
- 先……再……：The cost of books had increased to 23% by 1991 before decreasing to only 9% by the end of the period.
- 尽管局部：The share price saw a dramatic increase from $35 in 1996 to $80 in 1998, in spite of a noticeable decrease in 1997.
- 最明显变化：This rise was particularly noticeable between 1999 and 2002, during which time the use of mobile phones tripled.

## 连接词（CC 分的来源）

**数据逻辑（数据与数据之间，只有 3 种关系）**：
1. 相似 → Similarly（副词, 逗号, 句子）
2. 相反 → While / Whereas（主句, 逗号, 连词, 主句）/ by contrast / on the contrary
3. 没关系（转移主题）→ In terms of... / With regard to... / As for...（介词短语, 逗号, 句子）/ noticeably, particularly

**时间逻辑（从头说到尾）**：
- 整体：throughout the period, by far
- 初始：starting at, beginning from
- 然后：over the following 4 years, before, followed by, thereafter, from 2020 onwards, since then
- 特定时间之后：2020, when/in which time it started to…
- 同一时间：while

## 静态图 Grouping 模板（数据接近、字数不够时救命）

1. **A 最大，其次是 B 和 C**：Soccer is the most popular sport at 25,000 viewers, followed by rugby (22,000) and tennis (17,000).
2. **A、B、C 都超过/低于某值**：Viewers for soccer, tennis and rugby all exceed 30,000.
3. **加 with 补充细节**：...all exceed 30,000, with the biggest number in tennis (33,000).
4. **A 的数据在某范围内**：The number of people watching tennis, golf and cricket ranges between 40,000 and 50,000.

## 表达多样化（段落间不重复的同义弹药）

**报告单个数据（以"过度种植占 28%"为例）**：
- A further 28% of land degradation resulted from over-cultivation.（考官句型）
- The third factor contributing to land degradation comes from over-cultivation, at 28%.
- Over-cultivation accounted for 28% of the causes of land degradation.
- The proportion of land degradation caused by over-cultivation was 28%.（被动）
- There was 28% of land degradation caused by over-cultivation.

**accounted for 的同义**：represented / constituted / made up / resulted from / was caused by / contributing to

**背景化（7+ 标志）**：
- 主语背景化：consumption → popularity（消耗越多 = 越流行）
- 谓语背景化：Over-grazing accounted for 35% → 65% of land degradation is caused by over-grazing and deforestation（数据相加 = 跳跃式思维）
- 数据组合：不是罗列 35%、30%，而是 65% of land degradation was caused by A and B

## 完整范文示例（Winter 单根线，全模板 ≥7 分）

原图见 `../assets/task1/line-winter-electricity.png`

> In winter, starting from 35,000 units at midnight, the demand for electricity increases to 40,000 units by 2 a.m. before decreasing to the lowest point (30,000 units) at 7 a.m.. Over the following 15 hours, the amount shows an upward trend to the peak at 45,000 units, in spite of a constant level between 1 p.m. and 5 p.m.. Since then, a dramatic decline appeared, falling to the initial figure.

拆解：starting from（时间逻辑）+ before decreasing（模板 9）/ Over the following 15 hours（时间逻辑）+ in spite of a constant level（模板 10）/ Since then + a dramatic decline appeared（句型 3）+ to the initial figure（终点=起点的高级表达）
